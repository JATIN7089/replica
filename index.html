How to play

The loop

  • Settle neighbouring tiles with food — more land, more building sites, more fame.
  • Work — assign villagers to Lodges, Farms, Mines and Markets. Idle villagers do nothing.
  • Survive — come Winter, food output collapses while consumption rises. Stockpile in Autumn.
  • Fight — build a Barracks, train a warband, capture enemy tiles or burn the Town Hall.

Winning (any one)

  • ⭐ 300 Fame — gained from settling, buildings, battles and Sagas.
  • 🪙 2200 Krowns earned with a Market or Trading Post standing.
  • 🏛️ Domination — destroy the rival Town Hall.
  • ⏳ After 9 years, the clan with the most fame wins.

Controls

  • Click / tap a unit to select, tap ground to move, tap an enemy to attack.
  • Drag a box to select many. Shift+click adds to selection.
  • Right-click to order selected units.
  • WASD / arrows or drag with middle mouse to pan · wheel to zoom.
  • H jumps home · Tab cycles your warriors · Space pauses.

Edge

  • Wildlands give hunters extra food; fertile land boosts farms; fish never freeze.
  • Altars make Lore; 40 Lore buys a permanent blessing.
  • Watch Happiness — low spirits stop population growth.
